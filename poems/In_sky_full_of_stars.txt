In sky full of stars, You seemed a pole,
But it was just another falling star, Which I fell for,
Eating up all the glitter it had just as a black hole.

In a sky full of stars, you seemed a beautiful moon,
But it was just the half that got set soon,
Turning all the light it into dark As on nights of no moon.

In a sky full of stars, you seemed a pretty star cluster,
But it was just some flies that twinkled far,
Erasing all the dots as if a duster.

In a sky full of stars, you seemed a beautiful dawn,
But it was merely another day with the night gone,
Skipping all the pages blank, as if they were never drawn.
