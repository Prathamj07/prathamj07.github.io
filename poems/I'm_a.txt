I am a book, a poem, a para, 
A chapter to remember, a story to share.

I am a coin, two faces, both seen,
One brings joy, the other unseen.

I am a bright day, a sun, a moon, 
A time to laugh, a time to swoon.

I am a dark night, where tears may fall,
But I'll listen and embrace all.

I am food, nourishing the soul's delight,
A bridge for apologies, healing with each bite.

I am a friend, a lover, a family, 
A shoulder to cry on, a hand to hold.

I am a memory, a dream, a hope,
A feeling, a thought, a soul.
