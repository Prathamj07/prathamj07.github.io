Oh, aaloo, how you blend so well,
You teach me to adapt to any spell.

Bhindi ,you're loved by just a few,
You show me to cherish those who value me too.

Salt, you're a precious gem, but with a cost,
You remind me to balance or else I'll be lost.

Salad, a mix of greens, so fresh and light,
Teaches me to nourish yourself right.

& Laddoo , sweet and tempting to taste,
Reminds me that sukoon should never go to waste.

Each flavor , unique in their way,
Teach me life lessons day by day.

So be it savory, sweet, or sour,
I live each taste, with each moment and hour.
